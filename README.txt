Maintenance Log – iPhone app version

1. Put all files in this folder on a free host (e.g. GitHub Pages: new repo > upload files > Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch "main").
2. On your iPhone, open the site's https link in Safari.
3. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
4. Open it from the home-screen icon. After the first open it works offline.

Your rows and lists are kept on the phone. Use "Save to device" > Save to Files now and then to keep an Excel backup.
